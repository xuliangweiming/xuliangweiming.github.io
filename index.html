I am Liang Xu. My current research examines wellbeing, care responsibility and participation in later life, with an interest in the social circumstances surrounding health and ageing.

My educational background spans accounting, advertising and marketing. My recent writing combines analysis of US survey data on older adults’ happiness with close reading of Japanese public texts on care and participation.

Care Responsibility, Participation and Consultation in Japanese Public Texts on Ageing

Preprint · Not peer reviewed

Drawing on three publicly available Japanese primary-source texts, this paper examines how vaccination assistance, care managers’ compensation demands and consultation on Respect for the Aged gatherings describe support, care responsibility and participation.

The analysis distinguishes expressing wishes, obtaining the conditions needed to act on those wishes, and commenting on arrangements. It considers how older people’s wishes and supporters’ efforts are recognised; consultation records alone do not establish that opinions influenced decisions.

Liang Xu · Published 5 October 2026

Changes across Periods in the Proportion of Older Americans Reporting Low Happiness

Preprint · Not peer reviewed

Using five repeated cross-sectional waves of the US General Social Survey, this study examines changes in self-reported low happiness among adults aged 65 and over, differences across marital-status and education groups, and sensitivity to survey mode.

The analysis identifies a higher adjusted proportion reporting low happiness in 2022–2024 than in 2010–2014. These are comparisons across survey periods; they do not track changes within the same individuals.
